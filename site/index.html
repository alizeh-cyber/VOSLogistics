VOS Logistics is Lime’s operations partner in Austin. We keep the city’s e-scooter fleet charged, in good shape, and where riders need it. Lime is the world’s largest shared e-scooter and e-bike company, with over a billion rides across about 230 cities. Lime recently went public, and as it grows, we’re growing with it.

We’re building a dependable local team and want people who can work hard.

What you’ll do

  • Swap batteries on e-scooters across your assigned zones
  • Move scooters to high-demand areas based on rider activity
  • Keep the fleet clean, safe, and ready to ride
  • Use the Lime ops app to track tasks and report issues

What we’re looking for

  • Reliable truck, van, SUV, or vehicle with a rack or trailer
  • A smartphone
  • Able to lift 50+ lbs regularly
  • Available at least 20 hours per week on a consistent schedule
  • Dependable, self-directed, and comfortable working independently

What you get

  • $30–$50/hr, paid weekly
  • Flexible scheduling: you set your weekly hours with us, with a 20-hour minimum
  • A steady role with a growing company
  • Room to move up as the team expands. Top performers will be first in line for lead and higher-responsibility roles.

Please note that this role might involve night and early morning shifts.
